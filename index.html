About

A short version

I came to bioinformatics the long way around — Linux systems and networking first, then machine learning, then mathematics to understand the formulas underneath. I stayed for the questions that sit between biology and computation.

My PhD, at Université de Sherbrooke, was on predicting genes in eukaryotic genomes using hidden semi-Markov models and machine learning. Now I build the infrastructure that lets us measure whether AI-generated scientific hypotheses actually hold up.
